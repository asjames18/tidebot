# Trademark and naming

TideBot's code is released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`), which covers the code only: the TideBot name and logo (`assets/icon.svg` and the README/social artwork) are not part of that grant, so a fork must ship under its own name and branding and must not present itself as TideBot or imply endorsement by the TideBot project; a fork may truthfully describe itself as "based on TideBot" or "a fork of TideBot" for attribution, and contributions back to this project are welcome under the same terms.
