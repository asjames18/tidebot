Open source · Self-hosted · Bring your own model

Your agents. Your models.
Your machine.

TideBot is an open-source autonomous multi-agent studio you run yourself. Bring any model — OpenRouter, Ollama, vLLM — connect 1,500+ apps through Composio, and give agents a local workspace or a cloud Linux desktop. No subscriptions, no lock-in.

Download for desktop Star on GitHub ★ Windows · macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon) · Linux — free, MIT-licensed, with automatic updates.

Built for people who build

Everything runs on your hardware, talks to the providers you choose, and stays out of your way.

🧠

Bring your own model

First-class OpenRouter (250+ models), plus OpenAI-compatible endpoints, Ollama and vLLM for fully local setups. Configure keys in .env or straight from the UI.

🔌

1,500+ app integrations

Agents act on the real world through Composio: GitHub, Gmail, Slack, Notion, calendars and more — tools are routed and permission-scoped per bot.

🖥️

Dual execution sandbox

Agents get an isolated local workspace on your machine, or an Orgo cloud Linux desktop with a full GUI they can drive remotely.

🌊

Obsidian glass UI

A WebGL-shader studio with streaming transcripts, chain-of-thought drawers, a ⌘K command palette and a real-time ShaderCanvas.

⏱️

Routines & proactive check-ins

Schedule agents on cron, let them watch files, commands or URLs, and check in with you on their own initiative.

📦

Self-hosted, always

Node + SQLite under the hood. One repo, one install, your data in your data/ directory — nothing phones home.

Open source, in the open

TideBot is MIT-licensed and built to be extended. The architecture, invariants and contribution flow are documented for humans and AI agents alike.

  • Full architecture guide in AGENTS.md — how the server, web studio and desktop app fit together
  • Contributing guide with a clear verification checklist — small, focused PRs win
  • Structured issue templates and a public Discussions space for questions and ideas
  • Security policy with private vulnerability reporting
  • CI builds every platform on every push — what ships is what passed
